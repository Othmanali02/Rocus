1. Model load: cold, then warm

Cold: click "Clear model caches", open DevTools, Network tab, tick "Disable cache", reload, then "Run load". Warm: untick "Disable cache", close DevTools, reload, "Run load" again. Repeat warm once more if you have time. Clearing removes only cached models for this origin, not your Rocus data.

Chrome Task Manager (Shift+Esc), "Memory footprint" column, in MB. Fill "before" just before clicking "Run load", "after load" when it says done, and the last pair after step 2. Then "Save readings".

3. WASM vs WebGPU, same machine

MiniLM: the app's Transformers.js 2.17 (WASM only) against Transformers.js 3.8 on WASM and on WebGPU, 100 embeddings each. Optional second button: Qwen2.5-0.5B through Transformers.js on WASM and on WebGPU, which is what a WASM fallback would cost. That downloads roughly another 0.5-1 GB from Hugging Face (my estimate) and can take a while.
